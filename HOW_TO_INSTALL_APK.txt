========================================
  HOW TO INSTALL ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE
========================================

METHOD 1: Transfer via USB Cable
---------------------------------
1. Connect your Android phone to your computer using USB cable
2. On your phone, swipe down and tap "USB for charging" or "USB options"
3. Select "File Transfer" or "MTP" mode
4. On your computer, open "This PC" and find your phone
5. Copy "QuickSell.apk" from Desktop to your phone's "Downloads" folder
6. On your phone, open "Files" or "My Files" app
7. Navigate to "Downloads" folder
8. Tap on "QuickSell.apk"
9. If prompted, enable "Install from Unknown Sources" or "Allow from this source"
10. Tap "Install"
11. Wait for installation to complete
12. Tap "Open" to launch the app!

METHOD 2: Transfer via Cloud Storage
-------------------------------------
1. Upload "QuickSell.apk" to Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive
2. On your Android phone, open the cloud storage app
3. Download "QuickSell.apk"
4. Open the downloaded file
5. If prompted, enable "Install from Unknown Sources"
6. Tap "Install"
7. Tap "Open" to launch the app!

METHOD 3: Transfer via Email
-----------------------------
1. Email "QuickSell.apk" to yourself
2. On your Android phone, open the email
3. Download the attachment
4. Open the downloaded APK file
5. Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted
6. Tap "Install"
7. Tap "Open" to launch the app!

========================================
  ENABLING "INSTALL FROM UNKNOWN SOURCES"
========================================

If your phone blocks the installation:

For Android 8.0 and above:
1. When prompted, tap "Settings"
2. Enable "Allow from this source"
3. Go back and tap "Install"

For older Android versions:
1. Go to Settings > Security
2. Enable "Unknown sources"
3. Return and install the APK
